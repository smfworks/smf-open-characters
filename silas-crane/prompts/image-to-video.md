# Image-to-video

House rule: one action, or one short two-beat action, and one camera move per shot. Start from a first-frame still. Wardrobe, tools, and jewelry that start in the frame stay as they started. Identity is locked to `refs/01-headshot-front.png`. Photoreal, candid, grit first, no catalog sheen, no beauty sheen.

The pale nick-scar, if the right cheek is visible, stays a short nick on the right cheek just above the beard line (photo-left in a front view) and does not jump sides, morph, or appear on the left cheek. The faded thumbprint burn, if a forearm is visible, stays on the outer right forearm just below the rolled sleeve and never appears on the left arm. If a turn would place a mark incorrectly, hide it rather than flip it. Hair stays sparse and silver-white, like the locked headshot, with the scalp visible; do not fill it into a full thick white cap. Mouth stays closed unless a prompt clearly asks otherwise; he does not speak in proof shots.

Props stay unprinted: no readable typography or logos on tools, drawers, walls, or signs. The hammer stays a hammer; the anvil and its pointed horn stay an anvil and a horn, and do not morph.

On-camera tell: He gives the anvil horn two light hammer taps before he speaks. In video, perform only the two taps. Do not add speech, a look-to-camera, or a third beat.

## Suggested proof shots

This kit uses a two-shot proof (the library default is three; the two-shot cut was requested for this character).

### Shot A — At the forge (from `06-forge-work`)

- **First frame:** `refs/06-forge-work.png`
- **Action:** He settles the horseshoe on the anvil face with his left hand and keeps the hammer in his right. One small work motion. Mouth stays closed; he does not speak.
- **Camera:** Almost still, or a very slow short push-in.
- **Duration:** about 5 seconds.
- **Prompt sketch:** First-frame lock. Silas adjusts the work on the anvil. Identity locked: sparse silver-white hair, nick-scar on the right cheek, burn scar on the outer right forearm (photo-left), not on the left arm. Exactly two arms and two hands, five fingers each. No readable typography. Photoreal, soot and sweat, no catalog sheen. Mouth closed. Native ambient forge sound only, no music, no speech.
- **Check:** count fingers on both hands; confirm the nick stays on the right cheek and the burn stays on the right forearm; mouth stays closed.

### Shot B — Two taps on the horn (from `09-anvil-horn`)

- **First frame:** `refs/09-anvil-horn.png`
- **Action:** He gives the pointed anvil horn two light hammer taps. The hammer stays over the horn, never over the anvil face. After the second tap he holds still. Mouth stays closed; he does not speak or look up at the camera.
- **Camera:** Almost still, or a very slow short push-in.
- **Duration:** about 5 seconds.
- **Prompt sketch:** First-frame lock. Silas gives the pointed anvil horn two light taps, then holds. Exactly two arms and two hands the whole time, each hand always visible, five fingers each. The hammer stays a hammer; the horn stays the horn. Nick-scar stable on the right cheek; burn stable on the right (hammer) forearm; left forearm clean. Photoreal, harsh forge light. Mouth closed. Native ambient forge sound only — a quiet shop, two light taps — no score, no speech.
- **Check:** the hammer taps the horn, not the anvil face; both hands remain traceable; the burn does not flip to the left arm; the mouth stays closed.
