# Stills prompts

Prepend the identity block in `../character-block.md` to every prompt, or start from the locked headshot as a reference image and keep the block in the prompt as well. The front headshot is the identity lock. Generate it first, then use it as the reference for views 02–09.

Photography, every view: a candid real photograph, mixed 35mm documentary and phone-camera looks, overcast and cold hangar light (sodium or fluorescent mixed with grey daylight), cold ridge weather, rotor-wash dust, sensor noise, imperfect focus, moderate pores, peach fuzz, slightly off-white teeth with natural variation, no beauty sheen, no sparkly catchlights, no lash polish, no CGI. Tired, wind-burned candids; a small real smile at most, in one or two frames; mid-task moments, not hero poses or recruiting-ad stances. Fictional original character, not a likeness of any real person. No manufacturer logos, no agency seals, no readable brand marks on the helmet, radio, mug, or aircraft. The helicopter, if shown, is a generic weathered utility ship with the fictional tail marking JX-07 only.

Visible locks must hold: small unadorned silver stud in the left earlobe only (hide in right profile, never flip); short pale nick-scar through the outer third of the left eyebrow only (hide in right profile, never flip); postage-stamp-sized faded dark-green three-peak mountain tattoo on the outer left wrist only, thumb-side, facing out when the arm hangs, clearly mountains, no stamp border, not the back of the hand, not mid-forearm, not on the right arm; medium chestnut-brown practical short crop, two to three inches and tousled on top, tapered at the nape, no streak, and from behind the same length ending at the nape well above the collar.
