# Voice and Suno

Juniper's voice is invented. Do not describe it as sounding like a named singer.

## Voice prompt

Copy as a Suno style or persona prompt:

> Clear anthemic indie-rock female voice, mid-20s, warm close-mic midrange with a little grain, a calm chesty alto that can lift into a bright open chorus, American diction without a cartoon accent, made for outdoor and adventure spots, never a pop whistle tone, never a Broadway belt. Live-room drums, wide guitars, dry vocal in the verse and a little hall on the hook, no auto-tune, no glossy robot sheen.

Shorter alternate:

> Mid-20s anthemic indie-rock alto, warm and a little grainy, calm in the verse, open in the chorus, outdoor-spot friendly, no auto-tune.

## Song directions

Use the voice prompt above, then pick one direction. Keep lyrics in first person or close third, high-valley rescue and ridge imagery, no real people's names, no real aircraft or agency names.

### 1. Weather window

Fifteen-to-twenty-second promo sting. A single electric guitar figure, a dry kick, and a short stacked vocal hook about a twenty-minute weather window. Tempo around 112–116 BPM. Hard stop at the end, no fade.

### 2. Hold still

Upbeat 30-second outdoor-brand or action-spot cut. Anthemic indie rock with a driving eighth-note guitar, tight drums, and a sung hook: if you hold still she will get you home. Tempo around 124 BPM. Open chorus, tight edit points at 8, 16, and 30 seconds.

### 3. Hangar mug

A mid-tempo hangar ballad for a longer cut or a pre-dawn montage. Clean guitar, a little organ or pad, brushed or light kit, faint hangar-room ambience. Tempo around 92 BPM. The vocal stays close and conversational on the verses, sung on a short refrain.

### 4. Tarn below the ridge

Radio-ready anthemic indie. Live-sounding drums, a rising chorus about the dark coin of water below the ridge and the honest wind. Tempo around 118 BPM. Good for a 60-second spot or a social loop with a chorus first.

## Lyric seed (optional)

A first line that fits all four directions:

> Knock once, hand off — the window's twenty minutes, I'm coming up.
