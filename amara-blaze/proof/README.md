# Proof video

`amara-blaze-proof.mp4` is a 16:9 cut of three image-to-video shots from the reference stills. Native ambient from the generations is kept; there is no score.

| Shot | First frame | Duration | Action | Camera |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | `refs/06-diner-shift.png` | 4.0s | She continues a coffee pour at the diner counter. The carafe stays a carafe and the mug stays a mug. Mid-shot her gaze lifts toward the camera while the pour goes on; the stream tapers near the end of the generated take, which was trimmed at 4.0s for the cut. | Slow push-in |
| B | `refs/07-lake-michigan-wind.png` | 5.0s | Wind moves her hair and scarf as her gaze eases toward Lake Michigan. Hands stay in her coat pockets. The tattoo starts visible on her left side of the neck (photo-right in this view); later it may be partly hidden by hair or scarf as she turns, and is never flipped. | Almost still, with the wind and her turn carrying the motion |
| C | `refs/09-record-shop.png` | 4.0s | She lowers the needle onto the record, takes her right hand off the tonearm (the hand stays visible), waits a beat, and a small closed-mouth smile lands. Take 1 was rejected because the right hand vanished after the drop; this is take 2. Trimmed at 4.0s, ending on the landed smile. A brief motion-blurred lift-off around 1.8s is a weak frame, not a fail. | Almost still, or a very slow short push-in |

The finished cut is 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 13 seconds (312 intended frames; the concat is 13.04s / 313 frames, a single-frame join artifact). Source clips are in `shots/`. Shot A and C review times are 0.00, 0.80, 1.80, 2.50, 3.20, and 3.90 to fit the shorter clips. Shot B uses 0.00, 0.80, 1.80, 2.50, 4.00, and 4.90.
