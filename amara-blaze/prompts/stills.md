# Stills prompts

Prepend the identity block in `../character-block.md` to every prompt, or start from the locked headshot as a reference image and keep the block in the prompt as well. The front headshot is the identity lock. Generate it first, then use it as the reference for views 02–09.

Photography, every view: a candid real photograph, mixed 35mm documentary and phone-camera looks, practical light, sensor noise, imperfect focus, moderate pores, peach fuzz, no beauty sheen, no sparkly catchlights, no lash polish, no CGI. Fictional original character, not a likeness of any real person.

Visible locks must hold: a small faded healed-ink outline triangle tattoo on her left side of the neck (photo-right in a front view); hide it in a right profile and in a rear view, never flip it to the right side of the neck (photo-left in a front view); if it cannot be placed correctly, hide it rather than flip it; the tattoo-free fallback is a green butterfly hair clip on the left side of her hair (do not invent or move a wrong-side tattoo). Small gold hoops when the ears are visible. Fire-red / bright orange-copper jaw-length wavy hair, not shoulder-length. Vivid natural green eyes. Outfit varies; the grey sweater is incidental, not a wardrobe lock. Props stay unprinted: no readable text, logos, or typography on diner signage, pie cards, album covers, posters, or a record label.

The locked reference set uses three-quarter views for 02 and 03 rather than true 90° profiles. Prompts below can still ask for a true profile; if a generation comes back as a three-quarter, that matches the locked kit.

Candid documentary portrait in mixed practical light, slight grain, no beauty-studio finish. Vivid natural green eyes, fire-red / bright orange-copper jaw-length wavy hair, small gold hoops, and the small faded outline triangle tattoo on her left side of the neck (photo-right in this front view). A rust-orange work shirt. Identity lock. 3:4.
