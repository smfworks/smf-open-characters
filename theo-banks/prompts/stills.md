# Stills prompts

Prepend the identity block in `../character-block.md` to every prompt, or start from the locked headshot as a reference image and keep the block in the prompt as well. The front headshot is the identity lock. Generate it first, then use it as the reference for views 02–09.

Photography, every view: a candid real photograph, mixed 35mm documentary and phone-camera looks, harsh practical light, sensor noise, imperfect focus, moderate pores, peach fuzz, no beauty sheen, no sparkly catchlights, no lash polish, no CGI. Fictional original character, not a likeness of any real person.

Visible locks must hold: small thin gold hoop in his right ear only, on the left side of the photo in a front view (hide in any view of his left side, never flip); short pale nick-scar on his right cheek, just above the jaw, same side as the hoop (hide in any view of his left side, never flip); postage-stamp-sized faded black chili-pepper tattoo on the outer left wrist only, on the right side of the photo in a front view, clearly a chili, no stamp border, not mid-forearm, not on the right arm; short dark fade with 1–2 inch loose coils on top, no streak, and from behind the same short length ending well above the collar.
