# Theo Banks

**Pronunciation:** THEE-oh BANKS

**Age:** late 20s

**Hook:** A high-energy street food-truck cook who works a late-night window.

**Genres:** upbeat promos, product ads, food spots, funk-pop commercials

**Signature color:** `#C2410C` (achiote)

**Tell:** He flips a spatula once, then points it at the camera.

**Status:** locked (22 Nov 2026)

## Story

Theo Banks runs a late-night window on a battered step-van called Late Plate, parked under a sodium lamp two blocks off the avenue. He cooks the way he talks: fast, generous, a little loud. Regulars know the rhythm — the slap of the spatula, the flare off the grill, the way he calls out names like he is introducing them at a wedding. He learned the work in his uncle's kitchen, and he still keeps a cheap radio on the dash, because the right funk track makes the onions behave.

On slow Tuesdays he feeds the night-shift nurses before they ask. He will not say that out loud. He will say the special is the smashed-pork torta, the salsa is hotter than it looks, and if you wait two minutes he will get the bun right. The truck is his, the debt is his, and the light over the window is always a little too yellow. He likes it that way.

## Visible locks

Only these four, and they should show in a wide shot:

1. A small thin gold hoop in the left ear only. Hide it in a right profile; do not flip it to the right ear.
2. A short pale nick-scar on the left cheek, just above the jaw, just above the jaw. Hide it in a right profile; do not flip it to the right jaw.
3. A postage-stamp-sized faded black chili-pepper tattoo on the outer left wrist only — clearly a chili, no stamp border, not mid-forearm, not on the right arm.
4. A short dark cropped cut: tight even fade on the sides and back, a short high-top of tight coils about two inches on top, no streak, no design. From behind it is the same length, ending well above the collar.

## Wardrobe

Faded black crewneck T-shirt, well-worn achiote-orange canvas apron with a rolled white towel at the left hip, dark indigo jeans, scuffed black kitchen clogs. A well-used metal grill spatula with a short worn wooden handle is his working tool. No hat in the lock portraits.
