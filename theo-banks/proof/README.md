| B | `refs/06-truck-window-night.png` | ~5s | He hands food out the service window in one motion; the customer takes it and he lets go, smiling. | C | `refs/08-roadside-break.png` | ~5s | He takes one sip and looks down the street. | Almost still, or a very slow push |

The finished cut is 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 13 seconds. Source clips are in `shots/`. Shot A review times are 0.00, 0.80, 1.80, 2.20, 2.50, and 2.79 to fit the shorter clip. Shots B and C use 0.00, 0.80, 1.80, 2.50, 4.00, and 4.90.
