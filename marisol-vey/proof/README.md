# Proof video

`marisol-proof.mp4` is a 16:9 cut of three image-to-video shots from the grit-pass reference stills (shot A retaken in the touch-up pass). Native ambient from the generations is kept; there is no score.

| Shot | First frame | Duration | Action | Camera |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | `refs/06-radio-room-night.png` | ~5s | She taps the ferry-token charm once against her collarbone; the hand comes off the token before she leans toward the gooseneck mic. The mic stays put; the headset stays on (left cup on, right cup hanging). | Almost still, very slow short push-in |
| B | `refs/07-harbor-deck-fog.png` | ~5s | She looks out into the fog toward the harbor mouth. Damp wind moves her hair. She stays at the rail. | Almost still, or a very slow push |
| C | `refs/08-close-listening.png` | ~5s | Her eyes close as she listens. She may give a very small settling of the headset. | Very slow push-in |

The finished cut is 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 15 seconds. Source clips are in `shots/`. Earlier proofs are in `../archive/round-1/proof/`, `../archive/round-2/proof/`, and `../archive/round-3/proof/`.
