# Image-to-video guidance

House rules for every shot:

1. Start from a still in `refs/`. That still is the first frame. Do not invent a new face or a new outfit at the start of the shot.
2. One action per shot, or one short two-beat action if both beats are small and physical. One camera move per shot. No wardrobe change mid-shot.
3. Prepend the verbatim identity block and keep the silver streak, left-eyebrow scar, philtrum-right mole, ferry-token necklace, and headset consistent.
4. Prefer 16:9. Duration 4–6 seconds. Motion should be modest and physical, not morphing or beauty retouching mid-shot. Keep the grit of the still: no sudden beauty sheen, no plastic skin mid-clip.
5. Check the last frames against `refs/01-headshot-front.png` for identity drift. If the face slips, regenerate from the same first frame with a calmer action.

On-camera tell: Before she speaks, she taps the ferry-token charm once against her collarbone.

## Suggested proof shots

### Shot A — The tell (from `06-radio-room-night`)

- **First frame:** `refs/06-radio-room-night.png`
- **Action:** She taps the ferry-token charm once against her collarbone, then leans slightly toward the gooseneck mic. The gooseneck stays a slender gooseneck and does not move on its own. The headset stays on (left cup on, right cup hanging).
- **Camera:** Slow, short push-in, or almost still.
- **Duration:** about 5 seconds.
- **Prompt sketch:** First-frame lock. Marisol lifts the gold ferry-token charm and taps it once against her collarbone, then leans a little toward the slender gooseneck microphone. The mic stays put. The headset stays on. She does not speak. Photoreal, identity locked, no beauty sheen.

### Shot B — Deck watch (from `07-harbor-deck-fog`)

- **First frame:** `refs/07-harbor-deck-fog.png`
- **Action:** She looks out into the fog toward the harbor mouth. Damp wind may move her hair. She stays at the rail.
- **Camera:** Gentle, almost still hold, or a very slow push.
- **Duration:** about 5 seconds.
- **Prompt sketch:** First-frame lock. Marisol looks out into the fog toward the harbor mouth. Damp wind lifts her hair. She does not turn all the way to camera, does not speak, does not leave the rail. Photoreal, identity locked.

### Shot C — Listening (from `08-close-listening`)

- **First frame:** `refs/08-close-listening.png`
- **Action:** Her eyes close as she listens. She may give a very small settling of the headset. No nod required.
- **Camera:** Very slow push-in, almost still.
- **Duration:** about 4–5 seconds.
- **Prompt sketch:** First-frame lock. Marisol's eyes close as she listens, the headset still at her left ear. Very slow subtle push-in. No talking, no beauty smoothing. Photoreal, identity locked.
