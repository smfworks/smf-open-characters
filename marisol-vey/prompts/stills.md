# Stills prompts

Prepend the verbatim identity block from `character-block.md` before the view-specific text. Use `refs/01-headshot-front.png` as the face reference for every still after the headshot is approved.

Photography standard: these must read as a real person's photographs, not cinematic stills and not a beauty campaign. Mix cameras across the set: some frames like a handheld 35mm documentary photo, some like a slightly messy phone picture. Use harsher practical and mixed light (sodium dock lamps, a fluorescent radio-room tube, a bare tungsten bulb, or a phone flash). Slight under- or over-exposure is welcome where it would happen naturally. Include real sensor noise or grain, mild chromatic aberration, imperfect or shallow focus, natural asymmetry, under-eye texture, uneven skin tone, and stray hairs. No beauty sheen, no glossy over-finished eyes or hair, no perfect catchlights, no airbrush, no CGI, no plastic skin.

Lock points: small round mole on the upper lip just to the right of the philtrum (her right); in a left profile it sits on the far side of the philtrum and must not appear on the visible lip — hide it rather than place it wrong. Silver streak at the left temple only (right profile shows none, or at most a hint hidden on the far side). Thin pale scar through the outer third of the left eyebrow. Postage-stamp-sized mermaid tattoo on the outer right wrist only, when the wrist is visible. Ferry-token necklace. Matching scuffed chestnut mid-calf lace-up deck boots in full-body views. Olive field jacket over navy cable-knit unless the view is the sweater-only variant.

On-camera tell, when it appears: Before she speaks, she taps the ferry-token charm once against her collarbone.
