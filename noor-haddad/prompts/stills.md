# Stills prompts

Prepend the identity block in `../character-block.md` to every prompt, or start from the locked headshot as a reference image and keep the block in the prompt as well. The front headshot is the identity lock. Generate it first, then use it as the reference for views 02–09.

Photography, every view: a candid real photograph, mixed 35mm documentary and phone-camera looks, hard fluorescent tubes and monitor glow, sensor noise, imperfect focus, moderate pores, peach fuzz, no beauty sheen, no sparkly catchlights, no lash polish, no CGI. Natural unpainted matte lips, no lipstick, no gloss, no balm sheen. Fictional original character, not a likeness of any real person.

Visible locks must hold: nearly-black hair in a slightly messy low bun at the nape, soft off-center part slightly toward her left, temple tendrils, not a high bun and not worn loose; a small dark mole on the upper right cheekbone (photo-left in a front view) — hide it in a left-side view, never flip it to the left cheek; a thin worn gold chain with no pendant, and tiny gold studs when the ears are visible. If a one-sided mark cannot be placed correctly, hide it rather than flip it. Wardrobe: a rumpled newsroom-cobalt shirt the color of #1F4EAD (deep saturated cobalt, not bright mid-blue, not navy), sleeves rolled, white undershirt, charcoal trousers, scuffed black loafers; a faded charcoal cardigan is allowed at the desk. Backgrounds use blank or abstract charts only: no readable typography, no faces on boards, no logos, and no stray silhouettes or reflections.

The mouth stays closed and neutral unless a prompt clearly asks for a small closed almost-smile. Count exactly two arms and two hands, each traceable from its own shoulder, five fingers each, at full size before accepting a frame. No other people in the frame.
