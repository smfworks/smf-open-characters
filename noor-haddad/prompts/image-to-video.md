# Image-to-video

House rule: one action, or one short two-beat action, and one camera move per shot. Start from a first-frame still. Wardrobe, tools, and jewelry that start in the frame stay as they started. Identity is locked to `refs/01-headshot-front.png`. Photoreal, candid, no beauty sheen.

The mole, if the right cheek is visible, stays a small dark mole on the upper right cheekbone (photo-left in a front view) and does not jump sides, morph, duplicate, or appear on the left cheek. If a turn would place it incorrectly, hide it rather than flip it. Hair stays in a messy low bun at the nape. The gold chain stays a thin chain with no pendant. Lips stay natural and matte, not glossy. The shirt stays newsroom cobalt, the color of #1F4EAD. Mouth stays closed unless a prompt clearly asks otherwise; she does not speak in proof shots.

Backgrounds stay clean: no readable typography, no logos, no faces on boards, no other people, and no stray silhouettes or reflections. The pen stays a red felt-tip and does not morph. Any "number" on a page must stay abstract or out of focus.

On-camera tell: She uncaps a red pen and circles one number on a printout. In this kit the stills split that tell across two shots: the capped pen is on the desk in `06-newsroom-desk`, and she is already circling an abstract color block in `09-red-pen`. Do not add speech, a look-to-camera, or a third beat.

## Suggested proof shots

This kit uses a two-shot proof (the library default is three; the two-shot cut was requested for this character).

### Shot A — Capped pen on the desk (from `06-newsroom-desk`)

- **First frame:** `refs/06-newsroom-desk.png`
- **Action:** She studies the printout, eyes moving slightly over the page. The red pen on the desk stays capped and stays on the desk. Mouth stays closed; she does not speak.
- **Camera:** Almost still, or a very slow short push-in.
- **Duration:** about 5 seconds.
- **Prompt sketch:** First-frame lock. Noor studies the page. The capped red pen stays on the desk. Identity locked: low bun, mole only on the upper right cheekbone (photo-left), gold chain, deep cobalt shirt, matte lips. Exactly two arms and two hands, five fingers each. No readable typography, no logos, no other people. Photoreal, no beauty sheen. Mouth closed. Native ambient quiet newsroom only, no music, no speech.
- **Check:** count fingers on both hands; confirm the mole stays only on the right cheek; the pen stays capped; no typography or other people appear; mouth stays closed.

### Shot B — Circling a color block (from `09-red-pen`)

- **First frame:** `refs/09-red-pen.png`
- **Action:** She slowly circles one abstract color block on the printout with the red pen already in her right hand. She does not uncap the pen in this shot; she only draws a small slow circle. Mouth stays closed; she does not speak.
- **Camera:** Almost still, or a very slow short push-in.
- **Duration:** about 5 seconds.
- **Prompt sketch:** First-frame lock. Noor slowly circles one abstract color block. Exactly two arms and two hands the whole time, each hand always visible, five fingers each. The pen stays a red felt-tip; the page stays abstract, no typography. Mole stable on the right cheek only; gold chain stays; lips stay matte; shirt stays deep cobalt. Photoreal, no beauty sheen. Mouth closed. Native ambient quiet newsroom only, no score, no speech.
- **Check:** both hands remain traceable; the mole does not flip or duplicate; no typography or logos appear; the mouth stays closed.
