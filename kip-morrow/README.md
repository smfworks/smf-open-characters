# Kip Morrow

**Pronunciation:** KIP MOR-oh

**Age:** 19

**Hook:** A skate-shop kid who builds small robots in the back room after closing.

**Genres:** tech, gaming, Gen Z promos, lo-fi and bedroom pop

**Signature color:** `#84CC16` (electric lime)

**Tell:** He flicks one toggle on his little desk robot and its eye blinks on once.

**Status:** locked (22 Nov 2026)

## Story

Kip Morrow is nineteen and works the register, the repair bench, and the late shift at a small independent skate shop. He grew up in the back of that shop, sweeping up grip-tape scraps and watching the owner true wheels, and when the owner started letting him lock up, he started staying after. The back room is his now: a scarred workbench, a clamp-on lamp, a coffee can of salvaged motors, and a soldering iron he bought used with his first paycheck. He builds small robots out of scrap — broken toys, dead phones, a skateboard bearing for a wheel — and he names none of them. He talks fast and dry, half-joking, and usually knows the answer before you finish the question. The LED band on his wrist was his first board that worked; he never took it off. The scorch on his hoodie cuff came from the second board. After every build, before he says anything, he flicks one toggle on the little robot and waits for its eye to blink on, and for a second the tired face goes quiet, like the thing just said hello back.

## Visible locks

Only these three, all on his left side, and they should show in a wide shot:

1. A small solid matte-black plug, about the size of a pencil eraser, in his left earlobe only. His right ear has no piercing. In a right profile the plug is on the far side; hide it, do not flip it.
2. A hand-soldered LED band on his left wrist only: a bare green circuit-board strip about one centimeter wide with five tiny lime-green LEDs and a few solder blobs on a plain black elastic strap. No logo, no lettering. His right wrist is bare.
3. A worn, sun-faded, pilled electric-lime pullover hoodie, completely unbranded, with a small brown-black scorch burn on the left cuff only.

## Identity

Filipino-American, 19, about 5 feet 7 inches, slim and a little narrow-shouldered. Shaggy dark black-brown mop, ear-length, with a messy fringe to the eyebrows and a few bleached brassy-blond tips at the fringe only. From behind it ends just above the hoodie collar.

## Wardrobe

The lime hoodie over a plain faded grey t-shirt, loose faded black work pants, and beat-up plain black canvas skate shoes with no logos or stripes. No other jewelry, no watch, no hat, no rings. Props stay unprinted: blank natural-wood decks with no graphics, plain black grip tape, a plain steel file, a plain soldering iron, a plain paper cup. His desk robot is palm-sized and hand-built: two wheels, a scuffed grey unbranded chassis, visible red and black wiring, one round eye LED that lights lime green, and one small silver toggle on top.
