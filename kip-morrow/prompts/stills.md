# Stills prompts

Prepend the identity block in `../character-block.md` to every prompt, or start from the locked headshot as a reference image and keep the block in the prompt as well. The front headshot is the identity lock. Generate it first, then use it as the reference for views 02–09. For any view with the desk robot, add `refs/09-toggle-tell.png` (or `06-robot-bench.png`) as a second reference.

Photography, every view: a candid real photograph, mixed phone-camera and 35mm documentary looks, harsh skate-shop fluorescent tubes with a greenish cast, a warm clamp-on workbench lamp, phone flash at night, mixed color casts, slight under- or overexposure, sensor noise, grain, imperfect focus, real pores, faded acne marks, faint oily shine, scuffed knuckles, no makeup, no beauty sheen, no sparkly catchlights, no CGI. Tired after-close candids; a small closed-mouth smirk at most, in one or two frames; mid-task moments, not hero poses or skate-ad stances. Fictional original character, not a likeness of any real person. No readable text, price tags, stickers, labels, logos, brand names, or printed deck graphics anywhere: blank natural-wood decks and plain black grip tape only.

Visible locks must hold, all on his LEFT side: a small solid matte-black plug in his LEFT earlobe only (right ear bare; hide it in a right profile, never flip it); a hand-soldered green circuit-board LED band with five lime-green LEDs on his LEFT wrist only (right wrist bare); a worn faded electric-lime unbranded hoodie with a small scorch burn on the LEFT cuff only.

In a true front view, the arm on the right of the frame is his LEFT arm. In a three-quarter view, have him face the RIGHT edge of the frame so his left ear and left arm are toward the camera. Say "LEFT cuff" and "on the cuff edge, not mid-forearm" every time the burn shows; the model otherwise puts it on the right cuff in a front view or mid-forearm in a side view.
