# Image-to-video

House rule: one action, or one short two-beat action, and one camera move per shot. First-frame lock from a reference still. Wardrobe, the ear plug, the LED band, and props that start in the frame stay as they started. Props stay what they are: the robot stays the same small two-wheeled robot with the same wires and one round eye, the file stays a plain file, the cup stays a plain paper cup, decks stay blank. No new wardrobe mid-shot. No logos or readable text mid-shot. Photoreal, identity locked to the headshot and the first frame.

Prefer about five seconds. Generated 1080p clips are often 1920×1088 at 24 fps; crop 4 pixels from the top and bottom to 1920×1080. Review late frames for softness and for locks (left-ear plug, left-wrist LED band, left-cuff burn, bleached fringe tips). If the action is complete and the hold only adds a growing smile or softness, trim rather than regenerate. Keep native ambient; no score.

The on-camera tell is one beat: He flicks one toggle on his little desk robot and its eye blinks on once. Start from a frame where the eye is OFF and his finger hovers beside the toggle, not on it. The eye lighting lime green is the visible result.

## Proof shots

### Shot A — The tell (from `09-toggle-tell`)

> The young man moves his raised index finger the short distance to the small silver toggle switch on top of his little hand-built robot and flicks it up once with a single small click. At that moment the robot's single round eye lights up a soft lime green and stays lit. His finger comes away from the toggle and he watches the glowing eye with a tiny tired smirk. Nothing else changes: same face, same shaggy hair, same black plug in his left ear, same green circuit-board LED band on his left wrist, same faded lime hoodie with the scorch mark, the robot stays the same robot with the same wires. Warm workbench lamp, dim back room, grain. Almost-still camera, no zoom. Native room ambience only, no music.

Result: the eye is dark at 0.8 s and lit by 1.8 s; it stays lit. His hand drops off the bench afterward and the closed-mouth smirk grows into a soft closed-mouth smile, so the shot is trimmed at 4.25 s to shorten the hold.

### Shot B — Re-grip (from `07-counter-regrip`)

> The young man draws the plain steel file slowly along the edge of the blank skateboard deck once, scraping the black grip tape clean along the edge, while his other hand holds the deck steady. Eyes on the deck, tired and focused, mouth closed. Nothing else changes. Harsh shop fluorescents, grain. Almost-still camera, no zoom. Native ambience only: the rasp of the file and a faint fluorescent hum, no music.

### Shot C — Night flash (from `08-night-flash`)

> Handheld phone video at night, lit by the phone's constant flash light. He takes one slow sip from the plain paper cup, lowers it to his knee, and glances off down the dark street, tired. Nothing else changes: same face, same shaggy hair with bleached tips, same black plug in his left ear, same LED band on his left wrist, same hoodie with the scorch mark on the left cuff, the deck stays blank. Almost-still camera, no zoom. Native ambience only, no music.
