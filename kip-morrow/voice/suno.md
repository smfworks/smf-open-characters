# Voice and Suno

Kip's voice is invented. Do not describe it as sounding like a named singer.

## Voice prompt

Copy as a Suno style or persona prompt:

> Quick, wry male tenor, 19, close-mic and half-spoken in the verse like he is talking to you from the workbench at 1 a.m., dry and a little deadpan, lifting into a light, easy, slightly breathy chorus without a big belt. Lo-fi bedroom pop: dusty drum machine, warm detuned keys, a clean electric guitar with chorus, tape hiss, a little vinyl crackle, no auto-tune, no glossy pop sheen.

Shorter alternate:

> 19-year-old quick, wry tenor, lo-fi bedroom pop, half-spoken verse, light easy chorus, dusty drum machine, tape hiss, no auto-tune.

## Song directions

Use the voice prompt above, then pick one direction. Keep lyrics in first person, with solder, back-room, grip-tape, and after-close imagery. No real people's names, no real brands, shops, or games.

### 1. Eye on

A 15–20 second promo sting for a tech or gaming spot. A single detuned keys chord, a soft click and a bright synth blip on the downbeat (the robot's eye), one sung line. Tempo around 85 BPM. Ends on the blip with a short tape tail, no fade.

### 2. After close

A 60–90 second lo-fi bedroom-pop track for a longer explainer or a devlog. Lazy drum machine, a chorused guitar riff, a hummable chorus about staying after the shutter comes down. Tempo around 78 BPM. Builds once with a second guitar and lands back on the riff.

### 3. Re-grip

A punchy 30-second Gen Z promo cut. Tighter drums, a bass line, quick half-rapped verse, a two-word hook. Tempo around 100 BPM. Clean edit points at 8, 16, and 30 seconds.

### 4. Solder smoke

A slow late-night instrumental bed for a behind-the-scenes montage: the lamp, the iron, the little robot on the bench. Warm keys, soft vinyl crackle, faint room hum, one hummed line near the end. Tempo around 68 BPM.

## Lyric seed (optional)

A first line that fits all four directions:

> Flip it on, wait a sec — there you are, little guy, blinking back at me.
