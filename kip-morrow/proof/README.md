| Shot | First frame | Duration | Action | Camera |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | `refs/09-toggle-tell.png` | ~4.25s | The tell. Trimmed at 4.25s to shorten the hold (the smirk was growing into a soft closed-mouth smile). Face stayed sharp. | Almost still |
| B | `refs/07-counter-regrip.png` | ~5s | He files the grip tape along the deck edge with a plain steel file while the other hand steadies the deck. Plug, LED band, and burn hold for the whole shot. Not trimmed. | Almost still |
| C | `refs/08-night-flash.png` | ~5s | Phone-flash night shot: one sip from the plain paper cup, he lowers it and glances off down the street. Face sharp at the end. Not trimmed. | Almost still |

The finished cut is 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 14.3 seconds. Source clips are in `shots/`.
