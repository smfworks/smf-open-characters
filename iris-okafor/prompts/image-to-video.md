# Image-to-video

House rule: one action, or one short two-beat action, and one camera move per shot. First-frame lock from a reference still. Wardrobe, glasses, and tools that start in the frame stay as they started. Props stay what they are: the laser pointer stays a small plain black pointer, the mug stays a plain unprinted mug, the console stays the same console. No new wardrobe mid-shot. No logos or readable text mid-shot. Photoreal, identity locked to the headshot and the first frame.

Prefer about five seconds. Generated 1080p clips are often 1920×1088 at 24 fps; crop 4 pixels from the top and bottom to 1920×1080. Review late frames for softness and for locks (grey right temple, right-thumb band, one pair of glasses on the cord). If a slow push-in softens the face after the action is complete, trim at the last sharp frame rather than immediately regenerating. Keep native ambient; no score.

The on-camera tell is one beat: She clicks her laser pointer off once and looks up at the dome. Start from a frame where the beam is on and her thumb is near the button, not planted on it. The beam going out is the visible click.

The pointer stays a small plain black pointer in her right hand, glasses stay hanging on the cord. Camera almost still. Native ambient only: dome room tone, a soft click. No music.
