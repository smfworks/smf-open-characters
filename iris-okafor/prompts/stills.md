# Stills prompts

Prepend the identity block in `../character-block.md` to every prompt, or start from the locked headshot as a reference image and keep the block in the prompt as well. The front headshot is the identity lock. Generate it first, then use it as the reference for views 02–09.

Photography, every view: a candid real photograph, mixed 35mm documentary and phone-camera looks, dim planetarium dome light, cool blue-violet projector spill and star-field glow, office fluorescents, a desk lamp, console LEDs, mixed color casts, slight underexposure, sensor noise, grain, imperfect focus, real pores and fine lines, under-eye texture, no makeup, no beauty sheen, no sparkly catchlights, no lash polish, no CGI. Tired late-shift candids; a small closed-lip smile at most, in one or two frames; mid-task moments, not hero poses or museum-ad stances. Fictional original character, not a likeness of any real person. No readable text, labels, logos, or brand names on the console, pointer, mug, projector, papers, or walls.

Visible locks must hold: short natural coils, one and a half to two inches, black-brown, with a palm-sized grey patch at her RIGHT temple only (left temple dark; hide the patch in a left profile, never flip it; same short length from behind); a thin plain silver band ring on her RIGHT THUMB only, on the thumb itself, with no rings on any finger; thin tortoiseshell reading glasses on a thin plain black cord, one pair only, usually hanging at mid-chest.

In a true front view, the hand on the left of the frame is her right hand. Say "right thumb" and "no rings on any finger" every time a hand shows; the model otherwise moves the band to the ring or index finger.
