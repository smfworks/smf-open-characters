# Dr. Iris Okafor

**Pronunciation:** EYE-ris oh-KAH-for

**Age:** mid-40s

**Hook:** A planetarium curator who runs the late show under an aging star projector.

**Genres:** science, education, homeschool content, cinematic ballads

**Signature color:** `#2E2A6B` (dome indigo)

**Tell:** She clicks her laser pointer off once and looks up at the dome.

**Status:** locked (22 Nov 2026)

## Story

Dr. Iris Okafor curates a small municipal planetarium that runs on an old optomechanical star projector, a borrowed budget, and her own late nights. She trained as an astrophysicist, spent a few years on survey data she never saw in person, and came home to the dome because she wanted people in the room when the sky came up. She runs the school matinees, the homeschool Tuesdays, and the Friday late show, and in between she is under the projector with a flashlight and a screwdriver, coaxing one more season out of a lamp that should have been replaced years ago. She talks the way a good teacher listens: slowly, with room for the question. Her reading glasses live on a cord because she lost three pairs in the dark. At the end of every show she clicks the laser pointer off once, and for a second she just looks up at the dome with everyone else, as if she is seeing it for the first time too. She will tell you the light you are looking at left before anyone in the room was born, and that this is not a sad thing.

1. Short natural coils, about one and a half to two inches all over, black-brown, with a palm-sized patch of grey coils at the right temple only, sweeping back above the right ear. The left temple stays dark. In a left profile the grey patch is on the far side; hide it, do not flip it. From behind the coils are the same short length and end at the nape.
2. A thin plain silver band ring on the right thumb only, on the thumb itself between the base and the knuckle. No rings on any finger of either hand.
3. Thin dark tortoiseshell reading glasses on a thin plain black cord, usually hanging at mid-chest, sometimes worn low on the nose to read. One pair only.

## Wardrobe

A slightly pilled deep dome-indigo wool crewneck cardigan over a plain charcoal-grey knit top, loose dark grey trousers, and worn black flat leather shoes. No lanyard, badge, name tag, or earrings. Props stay unprinted: a small plain matte-black pen-style laser pointer with a single button, a plain off-white mug, a plain flashlight. The star projector is a generic old lens-studded sphere on a scuffed pedestal; the operator console has unlabeled buttons, faders, and LEDs.
